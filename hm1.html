Aethelgard Institute of Arcane Engineering

Aethelgard Institute of Arcane Engineering is a breathtaking fictional college hidden deep within the mist-shrouded peaks of the Whispering Mountains. Established in the early solar era, the campus seamlessly blends ancient, gothic stone architectures with soaring towers of brass and glass, powered completely by harnessed ley-line energy. Students from all corners of the realm gather here to master the intricate balance between volatile primal magic and advanced clockwork mechanics. The institute is famous for its massive, ever-shifting labyrinthine library—where books float effortlessly through the air—and its treacherous levitating courtyard, which hosts high-stakes, magical anti-gravity duels between rival houses.

Courses

Foundational Clockwork & Ley-Line Wiring

  • An introductory course teaching students how to safely lace standard copper gears with dormant mana currents to power basic machinery without causing accidental explosions.

Automata Psychology & Spirit Binding

  • A hands-on laboratory class focused on constructing mechanical constructs and successfully binding benign spirits to their clockwork cores to grant them basic intelligence.

Chrono-Spatial Locomotive Design

  • A senior-level capstone course where students collaborate to build experimental vehicles capable of safely navigating localized time loops and stable paradoxes.
